Also on the truck

  • Heated waterCuts through frozen ground for winter daylighting, watermain breaks and frost digs.
  • Low-clearance deflectorRemovable deflector plate for work under overhead restrictions or in tight corners.
  • Tri-drive chassisThree driven axles for traction on shoulders, boulevards and winter sites, so the truck stays put while the hose does the work.
  • Owner-led crewsThe owner quotes the job and stays accountable for it on site. A swamper rounds out the crew when the scope calls for it.
  • Spoil hauled, not leftSlurry goes to a licensed receiving facility under our MECP EASR registration, shown as its own line on your quote.

What we need from you

  • A valid Ontario One Call ticketWe confirm it before we mobilize, and we can arrange private locates for anything the public locate does not cover.
  • Room to stage the truckA full-size tri-drive truck needs a spot near the dig; the remote hose covers the rest when it cannot park close.
  • A water fill point for long digsHydrant permit or on-site supply; we carry 6,000 L and plan the fills around your schedule.
  • A site contact and PO or approvalSomeone we can call on arrival, and whatever your accounts team needs on the invoice.
