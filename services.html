01 / Daylighting & Potholing

See Exactly What's Buried Before You Dig

Pinpoint the depth, alignment and condition of buried gas, hydro, fibre and water lines with clean, precise potholes — so design teams, drillers and excavators work from fact, not from a paint mark.

  • Positive visual confirmation of utility depth and location
  • Ideal ahead of directional drilling, piling and design surveys
  • Photo and depth documentation delivered with every pothole
Slot trench · boulevard service line
02 / Slot Trenching

Narrow, Precise Trenches With Minimal Restoration

When you need a clean channel for conduit, cable or fibre, hydrovac slot trenching cuts exactly the width you specify — no over-dig, no torn-up boulevards, and far less surface for your crews to restore afterward.

  • Consistent trench width and depth, even around obstacles
  • Less surface damage means lower restoration cost for your crews
  • Safe to run alongside congested existing utilities
High-capacity vac truck on site
03 / Vac & Debris Removal

Powerful Vacuum Services for Wet or Dry Material

Clear catch basins, oil-grit separators (Stormceptor and similar units), sumps, wet wells, tanks and accumulated spoil quickly and cleanly. Our high-flow vacuum handles slurry, mud and standing water without sending a worker into a confined space.

  • Catch basin, sump, wet well and tank clean-outs
  • Spoil and slurry removal that keeps active sites clear
  • Responsible offload and disposal of collected material
Heated dig through frost line
04 / Frost & Cold-Weather Excavation

Ontario Winters Don't Shut Us Down

When ground freezes a metre deep and conventional digging stalls, our heated-water systems slice through frost line to keep emergency repairs and winter builds moving — safely, all season.

  • Heated water cuts deep frost other crews can't break
  • Keeps winter watermain and utility repairs on schedule
  • No thawing delays, no thermal damage to nearby lines
Extended hose · restricted-access dig
05 / Remote Hose Excavation

Reach the Digs Other Trucks Can't

Not every excavation sits where a truck can park. With extended remote hose, we dig hundreds of feet from the unit — into backyards, between buildings, down easements and even indoors — without driving heavy equipment across a finished or sensitive site.

  • Excavate well away from where the truck can stage
  • Ideal for backyards, tight lots, easements and indoor digs
  • Protects landscaping, hardscape and finished surfaces from heavy traffic
Private locate + visual confirm

Locate, Then Prove It

We pair private utility locating with hydrovac daylighting so you don't just have a mark on the grass — you have the line, exposed and confirmed. Especially critical for unregistered private services.

  • Private locates for services beyond Ontario One Call scope
  • Visual verification removes guesswork before machine dig
  • Reduces strike risk and costly utility damage claims

08 / Water Box & Curb Stop Services

Expose, Repair & Thaw Water Boxes — Damage-Free

Municipal water crews rely on hydrovac to work around curb stops and water service boxes without cracking the box or nicking the line. We expose, locate, repair, replace and raise water boxes to grade — and in winter, thaw frozen water services with heated water.

  • Locate and daylight buried water boxes & curb stops, strike-free
  • Repair, replace and raise water boxes to grade
  • Thaw frozen water services & boxes with heated water, all winter
  • Built for municipal water-service maintenance & renewal programs

Good to Know

Hydrovac, Answered

Hydrovac uses pressurized water to break up soil and a powerful vacuum to remove the resulting slurry into an onboard tank. Because nothing mechanical touches the ground, it's the safest way to dig around buried utilities — no blades, no augers, no strikes.
