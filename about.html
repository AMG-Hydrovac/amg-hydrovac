Who We Are

Ontario's Careful Diggers

AMG Hydrovac is built on a simple idea: the most valuable thing on any job site is what's already in the ground — and everything we do is designed to protect it.

Our Story

Operator-Led. Standards-Driven.

AMG Hydrovac is owner-led: the owner quotes your job and stays accountable for it on site, backed by 6+ years of hands-on hydrovac experience on Ontario sites. $5M general liability, WSIB registered, Ontario One Call locates confirmed before we mobilize, and a photo-and-depth record handed over on every dig.

Hydro excavation is the only thing we do. That focus shows up in our equipment, our locate discipline, and the documentation we hand over on every dig. Every job gets the attention, care and accountability the big outfits reserve for their biggest clients.
