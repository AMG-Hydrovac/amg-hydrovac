Straight Answers

Privacy

Short version: we only collect what you send us, we use it to respond to you, and we don't sell or share it. Here's the whole policy in plain language.

What we collect

When you submit our quote form, we receive what you type: your name, company, email, phone number, the service and region you select, and your project details. If you call or email us, we keep normal business records of that correspondence. This website uses the Google Ads tag to measure whether our ads lead to quote requests and taps on our phone number; that tag sets advertising-measurement cookies. We run no other analytics or trackers.

How it's used

Quote requests go straight to our estimating inbox so we can respond to you — that's it. Form delivery is handled by Web3Forms, the service that relays the submission to our email. We keep quotes and job correspondence as ordinary business records (estimates, scheduling, invoicing).

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for anyone else's marketing. We don't add you to mailing lists you didn't ask for.

Questions or removal

Want to know what we have on file, or want it deleted? Email info@amghydrovac.ca or call 1-888-482-4504 and we'll take care of it.
